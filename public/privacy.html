Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Host Control Center handles authentication data, apartment operations data, and information retrieved through the Google Sheets API.

1. Information we process

AuthenticationGoogle/Firebase authentication information such as account email, display name, and authentication identifiers needed to sign in and enforce access roles.
Property operationsApartment names, Wi-Fi details, check-in instructions, parking guides, agent/property-manager information, policy notes, cleaner prices, and access roles stored in the application's Firebase environment.
Google SheetsSpreadsheet title, sheet names, and cell values necessary to calculate inventory status and shortage reports after a user explicitly connects Google Sheets.
Local browser cacheThe selected spreadsheet ID, shortage terms, and a recent inventory snapshot may be stored in the user's browser local storage to improve continuity and preserve the most recent view.

2. How Google user data is used

Host requests the Google Sheets read-only scope solely to retrieve data required for the Inventory, Alerts, and Cleaner features. The application makes Google Sheets API requests from the authenticated user's browser.

  • The Sheets OAuth access token is held in application memory for the active connection and is not intentionally persisted by Host in Firestore or browser local storage.
  • Host does not use the Sheets permission to modify, delete, or create spreadsheet data.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale.
  • Google user data is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the application's requested functionality or comply with law.
Host Control Center's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Why we process information

Information is processed to authenticate authorized users, enforce role-based access, display and maintain property operations data, retrieve inventory status, prepare shortage and cleaner messages, generate invoices, and provide the specific functionality requested by the user.

4. Storage and retention

Operational records may remain in Firebase until they are updated or removed by an authorized administrator. Browser-cached inventory information remains on the user's device until it is overwritten or the user clears browser/site storage. Authentication sessions and Google authorization are also subject to the retention and revocation controls provided by Google and Firebase.

5. Security

Host uses authenticated access and role-based Firestore rules for protected application data. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security, so authorized users should protect their Google accounts and devices and should not share sensitive operational information with unauthorized parties.

6. Third-party services

Host relies on services including Google Sign-In/Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sheets API, and GitHub Pages. Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Your choices and controls

  • You can choose not to connect Google Sheets; other application features may remain available depending on your access role.
  • You can revoke Host's Google account authorization from your Google Account permissions.
  • You can clear locally cached Host data through your browser's site-data controls.
  • Authorized administrators can update or remove application access and operational records.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Host's functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
